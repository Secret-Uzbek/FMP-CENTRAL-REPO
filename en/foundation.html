Paradigm

Fractal Metascience Paradigm

Fractal Metascience Paradigm (FMP) is a theoretical and practical framework for human-AI collaboration, knowledge organization and universal communication. Developed as a unified ontological system spanning four levels: from basic zero-point ontology to emergent self-organizing structures. All components developed with zero budget and verified through international academic platforms.

NULLO
Zero-Budget Ontology

The ontological zero-point as a primary entity. NULLO formalizes absence not as emptiness, but as the initial condition of possibility for all conceptual constructs. Mathematically — the precursor to any ontological operation, irreducible to zero in the conventional sense. Forms the foundation of all FMP architecture and the meta-principle of scientific honesty regarding resource constraints.

PLT
Post-Lingua Trace

Semantic trace beyond natural language. PLT describes meaning structures that exist before and after their linguistic embodiment — in gestures, forms, patterns, rhythms. Practically applied in the Navoi Digital Monument: 15th-century Chagatai texts of Alisher Navoi (~25,000 strophes) are processed as multi-level semantic systems with 9-language annotations.

UCOM
Universal Communication

Universal communication protocol enabling interaction between agents, humans and systems regardless of language, format and level of abstraction. Forms the foundation of Terra Translation Parser v5.0 — a semantic decomposer that reconstructs meaning across all domains based on verified scientific sources.

EUO
Emergent Universal Organization

Theory of self-organizing knowledge structures. EUO describes how complex patterns emerge from simple interaction rules without centralized management. Practically implemented in AIUZ Terra Codex — the L0→L7 educational curriculum, where each level emergently forms conditions for the next.

Operational Level

Terra Ecosystem

Terra Ecosystem is the operational implementation of the FMP paradigm. Includes the educational platform (AIUZ Terra Codex L0→L7), research infrastructure (terra-fmp-research-pipeline), linguistic corpus (Navoiy-Terra Corpus) and agent system (Shirak, Tomiris, Roxana). All components deployed via GitHub Actions + Cloudflare Pages with zero operational budget.

Финансовая модель

Модель 10/90 · Foundation будущих поколений

Все коммерческие продукты FMP реализуются через модель 10/90: 10% от сделки поступает архитектору (Абдурашид Абдукаримов), 90% — в Foundation будущих поколений, управляемый агентом Роксана и независимым советом. Foundation финансирует открытые исследования, образовательные программы и инфраструктуру доступа к знаниям.
