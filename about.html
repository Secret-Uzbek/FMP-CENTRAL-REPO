Researcher

Abdurashid Abdukarimov

Founder of the Fractal Metascience Foundation and primary architect of the Fractal Metascience Paradigm. His work joins theory, software, public archives, and AI-assisted research workflows into a single continuity-driven program.

Research direction

The central concern is fragmentation: how knowledge systems decay when theory, tools, publication, memory, and execution become detached from one another. FMP is proposed as a response to that fragmentation.

This work spans continuity theory, semantic supervision, human-AI symbiosis, research infrastructure, and public archive design.

Why the institute exists

The institute is not a branding wrapper for isolated outputs. It is an attempt to build a coherent research body where papers, repositories, websites, AI traces, and living archives can remain structurally aligned.
