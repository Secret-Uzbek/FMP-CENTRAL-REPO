Biography

Abdurashid Abdukarimov is an independent researcher and conceptual architect at the intersection of linguistic-culture, international development, and advanced meta-scientific frameworks. His work includes the Fractal Metascience Paradigm (FMP), NULLO, PLT, UCOMM, and the Terra ecosystem.

Employment: Uzbek State University of World Languages, Swiss Embassy in Uzbekistan, Konrad Adenauer Stiftung, GTZ/GIZ, Uzbek Football Federation, Goethe Institut, and more.

Education: Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, Uzbek State University of World Languages.

Keywords: Nullo, Fractal Meta Science, Post lingua trace, fractal epistemology, transdisciplinary integration.
