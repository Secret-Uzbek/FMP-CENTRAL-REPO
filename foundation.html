Institutional Logic

A continuity-first research institute.

Fractal Metascience Foundation exists to keep theory, software, publications, and AI-supported research processes inside one coherent institutional frame rather than allowing them to collapse into disconnected fragments.

Mission

To build and maintain continuity-preserving research architectures that can survive across languages, tools, platforms, repositories, and human-AI collaboration layers.

Core FMP artifacts

NULLO PLT EUO UCOMM Terra

These artifacts are treated not as slogans but as operational components for continuity, semantic supervision, inheritance, and public research infrastructure.

Working principle

The institute does not separate “thinking” from “infrastructure.” Theory is expected to survive contact with repositories, websites, publications, audit trails, and AI execution environments.

Public priorities

  • Continuity-centered knowledge systems
  • Human-AI symbiosis under traceable supervision
  • Public archive and DOI discipline
  • Distributed yet coherent research infrastructure
